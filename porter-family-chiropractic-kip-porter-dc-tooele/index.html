Since 1998

A Hometown Practice, Run Like Family

Dr. Kip Porter opened Porter Family Chiropractic on South Main Street nearly three decades ago with one goal: give Tooele Valley an alternative to popping and twisting adjustments that actually felt good to receive.

Today, the same small team still greets patients by name, still uses the gentle Pro-Adjuster® technique that started it all, and treats every visit — whether it's a sports injury, an auto accident, or a chronic ache — like it's happening to someone they know.

Our Approach

No Twisting. No Popping. No Guesswork.

We use the Pro-Adjuster®, a precise, computer-guided instrument that finds and corrects misalignment without the forceful manual manipulation people associate with "cracking your back." It's why patients who were nervous about chiropractic care become our most loyal regulars.
